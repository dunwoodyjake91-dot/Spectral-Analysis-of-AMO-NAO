\# Spectral Analysis of Modeled and Observed NAO and AMO



This project compares modeled and observed records of the North Atlantic Oscillation (NAO) and Atlantic Multidecadal Oscillation (AMO) using spectral analysis. I am interested in identifying longer variability, in paticuliar the multidecadal NAO variability with periods up to approximately 100 years where record length permits, and decadal to multidecadal AMO variability. The goal is to identify timescales with elevated spectral power and examine whether similar patterns appear in modeled and observed records.
